* Conference version (36d156218)

*If* you have hardened the loop's constructed core (R1--R16) and want the machinery itself to be observable while it runs live, because every pathology so far --- wedges, livelocks, mixed code images, silently lost wakes --- was discovered by post-hoc archaeology on a completed broken run. *However* the loop's observation channel is task-level only: it observes the world it acts /on/ (job outcomes, verdicts, witnesses, QA), while its own machinery is a hidden state with no likelihood mapping; and simulation tests of the known invariants pass by construction the moment the known bugs are fixed, so neither channel can notice the failure modes nobody has imagined yet. *Then* weave a supplementary harness of invariant tripwires around the live run at its phase boundaries: each wire is a near-infinite-precision prior over /machine trajectories/ (turn conservation, ledger closure, review-commit provenance binding, append-only repair stores and their status lattice, wedge and livelock detectors, wall-clock overrides, loaded-code/file-code coherence, and an environment-alphabet wire on which any unknown job state trips rather than defaulting); a trip is surprisal that belief updating cannot explain away, and the mandated epistemic action is /freeze, record, park, summon/ --- write the frozen state as a durable report, open a typed stop-line through the loop's own repair machinery, park an investigation join and summon the outer reviewer, on a fail-safe ladder that only ever degrades toward recording, never toward blocking the run. Calibrate before arming: the wires must /retro-trip/ on the known incident corpus (a wire that does not fire on the known past is a dud laser), and every closed incident thereafter must deposit either a new wire or an entry on an explicit blind-spot map. *Because* this is interoception, not vision: like any sensorium it admits no completeness theorem --- it gets identifiability checks, a measured coverage number, and a blind-spot map instead --- but an organism with only a hand (R16) and a preference model (C) learns of poison from the stomach ache, and each hotfix repairs one meal; an organism with interoceptive nerves notices at the moment of violation, with the state still warm, and every incident leaves it permanently more observant. The trip stream is also precision evidence about the machine itself (R7): a wire whose trips keep being blessed as model revisions demotes itself down the action ladder, and a genuine trip should lower the system's confidence in its own machinery until the finding is discharged --- the noticing feeding the feeling.

/Operational witness (as of 2026-07-16; the counts below are that date's, and the wire set has since grown --- 13 catalogued and 12 enabled at the time of writing, retro-tripping 5/5):/ eleven wires, specified from the fix-ledgers of the loop's own review history, retro-tripped 4/4 on the reconstructed incident corpus (the late-author artifact replayed from the real phase ledger; synthetic terminal-wedge, rejection-livelock, and mixed-image cases). The channel's first live catch was --- as the no-self-certification lore predicts --- about itself: fifteen shadow-mode trips from one wire whose composition baseline had been captured mid-load, an observation of emptiness mistaken for an empty observation; the repair (a missing observation admits the first real one, exactly once) is a likelihood-mapping correction, made at zero cost /because/ shadow mode. Two subsequent armed runs --- including a full author--review--reject cycle and an operator sequencing error that the loop typed as an environmental hold and absorbed without wedging --- produced zero trips and zero false alarms. Over the following armed runs the loop then closed a three-week repair lineage through the front door: a cross-species adversarial refinement (claude author, codex reviewer) whose four strictly-narrowing rejections --- no reader boundary, a reader-retention race, a publisher-interleaving race reproduced deterministically by the reviewer --- terminated in a grounded change, with a new wire (author--artifact binding, chartered from a live incident in which the pipeline reviewed a sha /narrated/ rather than /measured/) guaranteeing that every commit reviewed was the commit authored. During the grounding run the museum spoke once: the wedge detector fired, factually correctly, on a lineage that was one run from convergence --- a true reading under a wrong precision --- and the summon path's roster check failed, degrading exactly one rung down the fail-safe ladder with nothing lost. Both were repaired the same afternoon through the contract's own /revise-the-model/ route: the wire learned to exempt convergence (fresh commits each round) while retaining the true no-work wedge, and the discharge was recorded on the ledger with independent-review evidence, like any other repair. The link from a trip to the commitment dial of R14 --- a wire firing should make the system less sure of itself --- remains chartered but not yet implemented.

A node can honestly be fully built and only analogically faithful; those are different admissions, and collapsing them into one scale would hide the more important one.

The wires do run at every phase boundary, not only at the start: emit-phase! calls tripwire/observe! on every phase event, and observe! evaluates all enabled wires (T1-T11 and T13; T12 is a chartered stub, disabled) against that event's record. (3) Until the 10-18 repairs, the stages after selection (target reading, squares, cascade execution, resume) emitted no phase events, so no wire ever saw them. The '13/13 tripwires clear' in each click's preflight is a separate pre-click readiness scan.
