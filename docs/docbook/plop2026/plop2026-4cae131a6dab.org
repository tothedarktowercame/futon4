* Conference version (36d156218)

*If* you want a genuinely closed action--perception loop: the agent acts, and the world it observes on the next tick is materially different /because/ it acted. *However* this is seductively easy to fake --- an enactor that re-runs its own construction and measures how faithfully it reproduced its own output produces a perfectly honest number over a quantity that cannot move (a mirror, not an act). *Then* close the loop only when the act writes a /typed, ungameable witness to a substrate outside the model/ --- an endpoint proven inhabited by a query derived from the authored bindings, not from builder claims --- /and/ that witness feeds the next belief. *Because* the point of a perception--action loop is that action changes the world and the changed world changes the next belief; if "realized" is coverage-over-reproduction, the world never changes and the belief never updates. /Revised against the record:/ the witness demand has its own measured failure family --- =:artifact-binding-mismatch=, four occurrences in the first cohort --- in which an /honest/ author has nothing bindable, either because the dispatched spec was too vague to act on or because the repair was data- or spec-shaped while the binding insisted on an in-attempt code commit; in the worst measured case the demand /induced/ a fabricated code change that independent review then rejected. The revision is that the witness demand must be /shape-aware/ (a deposit or a specification can be the typed, externally-checkable artifact), or the guard against fabrication becomes a generator of it.

* Current version (118b68957)

*If* you want a genuinely closed action--perception loop: the agent acts, and the world it observes on the next tick is materially different /because/ it acted. *However* this is seductively easy to fake --- an enactor that re-runs its own construction and measures how faithfully it reproduced its own output produces a perfectly honest number over a quantity that cannot move (a mirror, not an act). *Then* close the loop only when the act writes a /typed, ungameable witness to a substrate outside the model/ --- an endpoint proven inhabited by a query derived from the authored bindings, not from builder claims --- /and/ that witness feeds the next belief. The witness demand must be /shape-aware/: a deposit or a specification can be the typed, externally-checkable artifact when the act is data- or spec-shaped, because a binding that insists on an in-attempt code commit regardless of shape punishes honest authors who have nothing bindable (measured as =:artifact-binding-mismatch=, four occurrences in the first cohort; in the worst measured case the demand /induced/ a fabricated code change that independent review then rejected --- the guard against fabrication become a generator of it). And /typed/ means typed over a population: every witness edge declares an enumerable domain and range, or loudly states that either cannot be enumerated, and a claim to cover the whole domain carries an executable coverage falsifier --- a four-entry map named =sampled-candidate-cleans= covers its declared sample 4/4 and claims nothing population-wide, where the same map under a whole-domain name passed shape checks while covering none of the 96 observed open-mission selections. A witness contract without this population check can certify a fixture as the live substrate. *Because* the point of a perception--action loop is that action changes the world and the changed world changes the next belief; if "realized" is coverage-over-reproduction, the world never changes and the belief never updates. /Status in this implementation:/ the enactor in this build is the mirror the pattern warns against. , lines 130--160 (at futon2 =e76c51c=), shells a fold engine and returns a typed in-model result --- "=:constructed-wiring=, =:no-construction=, or =:enactment-failed=" --- a construction /inside/ the model, not an effect outside it; two independently role-played endpoints reached that conclusion from opposite sides, R16 finding no outward action and R2 refusing an enactor's wiring as /self/-report under the R9 verdict. The pattern named this failure in advance, and the implementation is on the wrong side of it.
