* Conference version (36d156218)

*If* the agent must choose among its /own capability gaps/ --- the proposer surfaces one "learn this action-class" candidate per class it cannot yet enact, and the loop must rank them. *However* gap classes carry no outcome statistics /by definition/: nothing about them has ever been enacted, so every honest posterior sits at its prior. This is measured, not hypothetical: on 2026-07-19, after a night of four consecutive externally-witnessed groundings, the leading five candidates were all learn-action-class entries at bit-identical scores (fourteen such candidates, every per-class posterior still sitting untouched at its prior, zero recorded updates), and the non-discrimination tripwire refused the pick --- correctly, since preferring one never-observed option over another would be invented, not inferred. *Then* give the choice ground the agent already stands on: partition the same embedded landscape the stack renders as its mission field into /action-class zones/ --- a Voronoi tessellation over class seeds embedded in the same space --- and value each gap by what its zone /carries/: structural load (enacted magnitude and summed turn-attestation of the features inside it), live demand (agent dwell-time in the zone, read from the running map), and posterior variance once months of repository history are described in capability terms and replayed into the per-class posteriors. *Because* capability in this stack is /stigmergic/ --- it lives in the built structure, not in agent biographies --- the structure's own load map is the only non-invented ground for preferences over learning; here the embedding stops being a generator of merge hypotheses and becomes the /coordinate system in which the self-model has ground truth/. The scope claim of R17\(^{\prime\prime}\) therefore sharpens: embeddings remain inessential to acting (R1--R16) and are fundamental /twice/ at the learning frontier --- learning what the model's concepts are (R17), and learning what the agent's capabilities are not (this pattern).[fn:1] A dimensional note with a principled core: the /operative/ partition should live in a 3-D reduction of the embedding --- not the print-flat 2-D projection (whose adjacency distortions would silently misfile boundary features) and not the raw high-dimensional space (where distance concentration leaves every nearest-seed margin uniformly thin and the partition uninspectable). Three dimensions keep Voronoi cells compact and the tessellation /walkable/ --- and the deeper reason is an acceptance invariant in the spirit of R9: the partition the operator visually accepts must be the /same object/ the machine's preferences run on, so the machine must operate in a space a human can actually inspect, with the high-dimensional nearest-seed reading retained only as a boundary-distortion diagnostic.
