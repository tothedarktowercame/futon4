* Versions: revised since the conference version
* Conference version (36d156218)

*If* you want the agent to discount low-trust evidence without discarding it, and to become stricter when outcomes are volatile. *However* without an explicit precision layer, evidence weighting becomes implicit prompt rhetoric and cannot be audited. *Then* keep a per-channel /precision registry/ --- one trust weight per kind of evidence --- keyed to observation features, and apply it wherever prediction error, scoring, or gating occurs. *Because* making precision explicit turns trust into a tunable control surface rather than an undocumented bias.
