# UKRN-S recommended design commitments, with supporting evidence — M-or-training-as-learning-system

Each commitment UKRN-S is recommended to carry forward, with its named
evidence basis (never a candidate framing and never WP rhetoric). Sources:
DERIVE decisions, invariant principles, coordination structures; VERIFY
prediction results.

**C1. Five-role equippage structure** (Training Manager, Evaluator, Convener,
Researcher-Advisor, Institutional Leads), each with explicit strengths,
limitations, and needs-from-others.
Evidence: MAP inheritance inventory (T1 delivery counts across 24
institutions; role codebook 12 roles / 61 action codes); the ~30× Lead
engagement spread (Newcastle 482 vs Glasgow 15 trainees); scenario tests A–C
all pass against the role structure.

**C2. A designated Evaluator is a Sorry-2 blocker and must be appointed
before bootstrap actions land** (part-time minimum, time allocated to
cross-institutional analysis).
Evidence: `EV_analyse` absent across all four ORP T3 focus-group transcripts
(role codebook); WP09 pilot infrastructure otherwise unused; Prediction 5
simulation (centralised D=0.78/A=0.76, 10/10; distributed viable at p ≥ 0.5,
fails at p=0.2).

**C3. The design rests on the role-and-coordination structure, not on any
architectural framing** — MVSG and the Krowne tetrahedron are set aside as
load-bearing commitments (usable descriptively); NPT and pattern theory are
load-bearing; active inference is instrumental only.
Evidence: Prediction 2 (role-coordination prior ≈ MVSG prior, both 10/10);
framing verdicts in `verification-record-framings.md`.

**C4. Three coupling responsibilities are explicit** — TM↔EV
(training-indicators), EV↔Convener (indicators-sector), Convener↔TM
(sector-training).
Evidence: Prediction 3 — decoupling is shape-determining (EV↔Convener loss
costs A 0.13; Convener↔TM loss costs D 0.08); the *Apex-becomes-node* failure
mode named in the WP modes section.

**C5. Funder catalysis is an explicit binding constraint** monitored and
surfaced by the Convener, with Researcher-Advisor cross-check on framing
legibility.
Evidence: MAP XENOTYPE finding (Research England £28m catalysed the 2021–22
research-culture work); Prediction 4 — funder-active raises both means with
dimension-specific variance (uniform D, divergent A), a dynamic the WP
framework did not carry.

**C6. The seven invariant principles hold continuously** (named role-owners
with allocated time; Evaluator-before-service-decisions; Convener commitments
with documented evidence; design changes trace to evidence updates; Leads
carry tactical participation unaided; no over-subscribed bandwidth; every
candidate framing carries a current verdict).
Evidence: each principle names the WP failure mode it forecloses
(Goodwill-absorbed, Reassurance machine, floating formalism,
Apex-becomes-node); VERIFY confirms the design holds across all simulated
configurations except distributed-p20.

**C7. The fidelity contract** (Preserve / Adapt / Drop) governs the ORP →
UKRN-S inheritance, with per-capability tripwires (ethics renewal and first
wave by Q3 2026; annual data-check of the population model; annual
role-coverage audit; Maria's form at scale by Q4 2026).
Evidence: MAP inheritance inventory; WP09 pilot verdicts calibrating which
indicators are operational; durability-first cross-reference.
