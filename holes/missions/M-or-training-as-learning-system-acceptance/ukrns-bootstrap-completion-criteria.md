# UKRN-S bootstrap actions with completion criteria — M-or-training-as-learning-system

The DERIVE fidelity contract (Adapt clause) translates the four WP-Prelude
bootstrap steps into Training Manager + Evaluator + Convener tasks with named
completion criteria. This record states each criterion explicitly. The
institutional capabilities being established are the sorry-closing capacities:
evaluation analysis (Sorry 2), tactical participation (Sorry 3), and the
behaviour-change evidence base (Sorry 1).

| # | Bootstrap action (WP Prelude step) | Role owner | Completion criteria | Capability established |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Publish scaffold** — training contract per course, expectation scaffold for trainers, ready-to-deliver definition | Training Manager | (a) a training-contract artefact exists per commissioned course with outcomes, audience, prerequisites, and explicit not-covered scope; (b) every commissioned course has a named role-owner with allocated time (Invariant 1); (c) no role's bandwidth is over-subscribed without explicit reallocation (Invariant 6). | Operational delivery substrate UKRN-S contracts against ("demonstrable", not endorsement). |
| 2 | **T1 wave** — run the evaluation wave | Training Manager (deployment) + Evaluator (analysis) | (a) wave runs at ≥6 institutions under KCL ethics MRA-23/24-42968, by Q3 2026 (Preserve tripwire); (b) every T1 evaluation is analysed by the Evaluator before results inform service decisions (Invariant 2); (c) Evaluator configuration chosen by the Prediction 5 decision rule (centralised if expected participation p < 0.5; distributed if p ≥ 0.5). | Evidence base for Sorry 1 (researcher-level behaviour change). |
| 3 | **Share evidence back** — cross-institutional findings returned to institutions | Evaluator (analysis) + Convener (sector translation) | (a) Maria's combined post-session form deployed at scale within Q4 2026 (Preserve tripwire); (b) a quarterly sector-engagement briefing is issued; (c) every sector-level commitment in it is made by the Convener with documented evidence basis (Invariant 3); (d) share-back calibration follows the `pheromone-trail-tuner` reading (signal, not noise). | Continuous cross-institutional evidence flow (Sorry 2) and tactical participation (Sorry 3). |
| 4 | **Peer-led extension** — institutions extend delivery locally | Training Manager (scaffolds) + Institutional Leads (local delivery) | (a) every Institutional Lead knows what their tactical participation looks like and can carry it without consultation each time (Invariant 5); (b) Leads supported with template language for local-context evaluation; (c) quarterly status check held; (d) each design change traces to a specific evidence update (Invariant 4). | Tactical institutional participation (Sorry 3) at member-institution scale. |

Standing review gates that guard all four: pre-wave protocol review (Evaluator +
Training Manager, Convener notified); pre-commitment empirical cross-check
(Convener + Researcher-Advisor); pre-service-change evidence-trace review.
