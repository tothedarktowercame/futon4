# Follow-on mission scoping: `M-ukrns-rollout` — M-or-training-as-learning-system

The post-bootstrap UKRN-S build-out, scoped as a follow-on mission with its
dependencies named (per the VERIFY exit and the "Open follow-on work"
paragraph).

**Mission.** `M-ukrns-rollout` — post-bootstrap build-out of UKRN-S: the
rollout of the training schema (`ukrn.org/training-schema`, ~50 topics across
7 categories), the coordinated indicators infrastructure WP09 calls for, and
the full peer-led extension, on top of the invariant substrate the bootstrap
phase establishes. Distinct from the bootstrap phase (ticks 0–30): build-out
is where programme-level patterns gated to terminal ticks fire.

**Dependencies (must hold before the rollout mission starts).**

1. **Prediction 1 result holds under real data** — the continuous-evaluation
   preference must survive T1-wave contact; if the falsifier triggers, the
   design reverts per the Sorry-2 closure mechanism before build-out.
2. **Sorry-1 evidence trail exists** — the T1 wave at ≥6 institutions must
   have run and been analysed (the closure mechanism completing is the gate;
   first results expected within three quarters of wave start).
3. **Evaluator configuration resolved empirically** — the participation
   fraction p measured across UKRN institutions (MAP Q3), choosing centralised
   (p < 0.5) or distributed (p ≥ 0.5) per the Prediction 5 decision rule.
4. **Bootstrap completion criteria met** — all four bootstrap actions in
   `ukrns-bootstrap-completion-criteria.md` have produced their evidence
   (scaffold published; T1 wave analysed; evidence shared back; peer-led
   extension standing).

**Adjacent follow-ons (separate, not blocking).**
- Real-data validation mission (fresh T1-wave evidence; depends on Sorry 1).
- `M-ukrns-coupled-systems` — futon-track research piece coupling nest and
  landscape; carries the AIF/multi-agent extension work deferred from this
