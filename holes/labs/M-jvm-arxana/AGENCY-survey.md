## Lexicon claims contradicted or weakened by code/runtime

1. **`kangaroo-pouch part-of agency` needs two relations.** Namespace membership is true (`futon3c.agency.agent-pouch`), but feature necessity is false: `FUTON3C_KANGAROO` defaults false (`agent_pouch.clj:46-49`). Record `in-namespace agency`; record `extends agency` as an optional, assessed feature claim.
2. **Federation existence is code-backed but live status is not.** The lexicon note says current use unclear. That remains correct: configuration and daemons exist (`federation.clj:96-105,1035-1162`), while `/api/alpha/federation/status` is not a route (404).
3. **Work orders are no longer merely unsettled.** The lexicon note called their relation unresolved; the route is live and returns open records, and terminal-job wiring is explicit (`http.clj:2691-2702`). Their exact relationship to Giraffe items remains unresolved.
4. **Agency clock has a stale substrate path.** The current clock store exists, but `clock_lineage.clj:27-33` still defaults to futon1a :7071. Any lexicon claim that all clock lineage persistence is live should be qualified.
5. **Test-registry is served by the Agency JVM but not an Agency namespace.** Its `part-of agency` claim is deployment membership, not namespace membership (`src/futon3c/test_registry*.clj`, routes `http.clj:12920-12935`). The same distinction applies to Drawbridge and most peripherals.
6. **`evidence-landscape` is a shared substrate/API, not a feature owned by Agency.** Agency exposes evidence reads and writes (`transport/http.clj:3699-3957`), but futon1b is the persistence authority and many non-Agency producers/consumers use it. Model this as `agency uses evidence-landscape`, not `part-of`.
7. **`emacs-repl` is an Agency client/surface, not an Agency feature.** Park delivers to it and Agency turns appear there, but its implementation is Emacs-side; model `emacs-repl uses agency` and `park delivers-to emacs-repl`.
8. **`multi-watcher` is co-resident, not part of the Agency namespace.** Its entry points are `src/futon3c/watcher/multi.clj:104-137,491`; model deployment in the futon3c JVM and its feeds, not `part-of agency`.
9. **`inbox-zero` is also co-resident rather than an Agency feature.** Its independent loop is `src/futon3c/inbox_zero/sweeper.clj:1032-1073`; it may use Agency/peripherals, but `part-of agency` overstates ownership.
10. **`pattern-search` and `pattern-catalog-search` name one user feature at this granularity.** The implementation entry is `agency/pattern_search.clj:150`; keep one canonical feature slug and treat the other as an alias unless a later design distinguishes retrieval from the catalog itself.

## Sessionista recommendation

Start with the least ambiguous case-sensitive forms: `Agency`, `invoke-job`, `bellback`, `whistle-stream`, `turn queue`, `parked-on`, `kangaroo pouch`, `work order`, `follow-up queue`, `Agency federation`, `mesh QA`, `pattern catalog`, `prompt line`, `operator turn`, `test registry`, `Drawbridge`, `Agency clock`, `promise record`, `agency hop`, and `artifact activation`. Before enabling the ordinary single words `bell`, `park`, `clock`, `queue`, `promise`, `warrant`, `peripheral`, or `federation`, build and review backlinks: the tiny samples above support `bell` and `park` as feature terms in recent agent turns, but do not establish general precision.
