## F4 as written (mission §1)

> Compose interpretation, grounding, correspondence, compilation, and
> projection 'clusions. Demonstrate that a 'clusion can itself be assessed or
> contradicted, and that criticism of one stage does not rewrite its source or
> unrelated stages. Executable dynamics remain behind capability and admission
> boundaries.

## Clarifications

1. **Chain**: F4 builds the D6 chain intent → candidate → decision → receipt
   over the **F1 fixture turns**. F3 correspondences are optional warrant
   input, not a dependency of the first F4 acceptance case.
2. **Composition**: an ordered composite 'clusion whose role-labelled
   endpoints are the stage records/'clusions, with a dynamics reference
   naming a versioned composition operator. It references a versioned futon5
   wiring diagram as topology evidence; topology is not copied into props.
   Existing AIF/exotype material (futon5 holes/labs/M-aif-tokamak/,
   data/wiring-rules/) is used only if its ports genuinely match; otherwise
   one minimal F4 diagram is made.
3. **Execution**: one harmless, bounded operation (preferably a read/query
   or projection refresh) runs after explicit capability and admission, and
   its receipt is recorded. Refusal without admission is also tested. The
   main acceptance is recursive addressing, assessment, and stage-local
   contradiction.

## Acceptance cases

- a composite 'clusion over the F1 stages is addressable, and each stage is
  addressable through it;
- an assessment rejects one stage; the source turn and the other stages are
  unchanged;
- a contradiction recorded against one stage stays local to it;
- one bounded operation runs only with capability + admission and leaves a
  receipt; without admission it is refused, typed.

## Depends on

F1 records; the core's 'clusion endpoints (allowed from F1); the existing
D6 compiler/admission shapes (futon3c arxana/compile/), which gain an intent
producer and the F1 intent types here, not in F1.
